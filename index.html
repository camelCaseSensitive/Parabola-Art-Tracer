Conic Trace MVP

Upload an image, click along one curve, fit it, accept the result, then copy the Desmos equations. This version is intentionally limited to curve types that paste cleanly into Desmos: lines, parabolas, and full circles for closed traces.

Trace

Click points along a single smooth section. 5–20 points usually works well. Zoom/pan now moves the image and trace together for close-up tracing. If you want a circle fit, trace a closed loop so the last point ends near the first.

Export

Accepted curves are exported as plain Desmos text. Open traces fit lines and parabolas with natural x/y restrictions. Circles are only offered when your trace is closed, and then export as a full circle equation.
